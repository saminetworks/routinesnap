A duty roster is a team document. It lists everybody — twenty names, a month of days, shift codes like A, B and C, and a legend at the bottom that explains what the codes mean. What you actually need from it is much smaller: *your* shifts, with the right start times, in your calendar.

## Scan the whole sheet, keep your rows

Upload a photo of the roster and pick "Duty roster". If the sheet has many names, type your name in the scan step — RoutineSnap keeps only your rows and ignores everyone else's. That works for a Japan baito shift sheet, a Bangladesh office roster, or a garments duty roster alike.

## Night shifts need care

The classic roster mistake is the night shift that crosses midnight: 22:00 to 06:00. Written as a single row, a naive calendar import puts the end time *before* the start time and the event lands on the wrong day. RoutineSnap treats an end time earlier than the start time as a midnight crossing, so the shift correctly ends the next morning — and the reminder still fires before the shift starts, not after it ends.

## Shift codes come from the legend

Rosters rarely print times. They print codes — A, B, C, or 早 and 遅, or ডে and নাইট — and the legend maps each code to hours. When the legend is visible in the photo, RoutineSnap reads it and converts the codes. If the legend is cut off in the photo, retake it with the legend in frame; guessing shift times is exactly the kind of mistake the confirm screen exists to catch.

## Reminders that match the job

A 30-minute reminder is right for a day shift. For a night shift, consider an hour — you want to be awake, fed and on your way before 22:00, not reminded at 21:30 when you are already asleep. Set the default reminder before you extract, or change it for all events at once on the confirm screen.

## When the roster changes

Rosters change. When a new one comes out, scan it again. Your old events stay where they are until you delete them, so delete the old month's shifts before importing the new ones — two copies of the same shift is the one mess a calendar cannot fix for you.
