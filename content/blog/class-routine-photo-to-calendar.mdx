Most class routines never arrive as a file. They arrive as a photo: a notice board in the corridor, a whiteboard after the first lecture, or a screenshot someone dropped in the class WhatsApp group. Typing all of it into a calendar by hand takes half an hour and a typo means a missed class.

Here is the whole workflow with RoutineSnap.

## 1. Take a straight photo

Stand in front of the board and fill the frame with it. Straight-on and well-lit matters more than resolution — a phone photo in daylight beats a tilted photo in a dark room. If the routine came to you as a screenshot, you can upload that directly; no photo needed.

## 2. Pick "Class routine"

The category tells the extractor what to look for: weekdays down the side, periods across the top, and a subject, room and teacher in each cell. Leave it on auto detect if you are not sure — it usually guesses right from the layout.

## 3. Check before you save

This is the step that matters. RoutineSnap shows every event it found in a list you can edit: title, weekday, start and end time, room, teacher. Low-confidence fields are highlighted in amber — check those first, especially room numbers, which are easy to misread from a photo.

Weekly periods repeat on the same weekday, so one row in the photo becomes a whole semester of events.

## 4. Download the .ics file

One file, every class, with reminders built in. Import it into Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook or Samsung Calendar and the reminders work with no extra setup. You can also add single classes to Google Calendar one at a time from the export screen.

## A few tips

- **If the board changed mid-semester**, scan it again and delete the old events — don't try to edit a whole semester by hand.
- **Check the year.** Routines rarely print the year, so RoutineSnap assumes the current one and tells you it did. If your semester starts in January, make sure the start date is right.
- **Blurry photo?** Retake it rather than fixing twenty events. A clear photo is faster than a long edit session.
